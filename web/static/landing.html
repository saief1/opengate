Features

Control at 3 a.m.,
not a report at 9.

Hard budgets & kill switch

Daily, monthly and lifetime USD caps per key, agent or project. Over‑limit calls are refused before they reach the provider.

Failover & smart routing

Ordered fallbacks per alias, retries with backoff, and an auto alias that picks the cheapest model that qualifies.

Every request, priced

Tokens, cost, latency, provider and attempts for every call, broken down by key, agent and customer.

Cost per customer

Tag calls with a header, apply markup, and sync usage to your Stripe billing meter.

Guardrails

Redact or block emails, cards, keys and other PII in requests and responses. Allow‑list models per project.

Open source core

Apache‑2.0, a single static Go binary. Self‑host with one command, or use OpenGate Cloud.
